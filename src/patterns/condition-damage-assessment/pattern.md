# Pattern: Condition Damage Assessment

## Purpose

Represent quality state, damage qualities, corroded regions, and corrosion processes for used products or components.

## Description

Use PMD Core `corrosion` for the process. Use AutoMatCE classes for application-specific qualities such as dent damage, paint damage, surface cleanliness, completeness, and quality grade. Attach qualities to the assessed component with RO `has quality`.
